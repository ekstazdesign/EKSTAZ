Elton’s words

From the outside a life can look polished. Up close it is a set of problems, and the fall begins when the combination is the kind you cannot carry. Winged, and still dropping, between order and chaos. Not because he leapt. Because he could not balance what was already in his hands.

Elton’s words move from the outside of a life to up close. You can do the same with any moment of your own. It doesn’t need to be heavy, and ordinary counts.
