# Sources checked for this iteration

The original site was read from `Happy-Harris/EKSTAZ` at commit
`d5aa6599e545198e33b5c0a19f97dc38df296a9b`. All 66 binary assets downloaded from
the deployed site were checked against their Git blob hashes; every file matched.
No artwork, wordmark or icon was redrawn or recoloured for this change.

The October 2026 `BRAND.md` and supplied EKSTAZ design-system PDF define the
identity, typography, colour, control shapes and motion constraints.

The additional [EKSTAZ Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1680Jk0KB1NIA1h_UPL6c2MmJb8i4ZjI4)
was inspected as source material. Its older handoff documents describe an earlier
business stage; they do not replace the current brand core or deployed site.
Several media filenames and inherited descriptions do not identify their actual
contents. A filename alone must not establish an artwork title or relationship.

## Relationships shown

- The hoodie drawing lists an embroidered red cross. Cross and the hoodie study
  are linked as studies of that motif; this does not claim a finished garment.
- The ornate cross motif appears in the background of magazine page 12. The
  Cross viewer links to that December 2021 archive page.
- Magazine page 10 contains the same red figure composition as Kenosha, headed
  “FINDING ORDER”, alongside two figure sketches and historical explanatory prose.
  Before thee uses the same core line composition in white, with different scale
  and placement. This was checked against the full artwork files, page 10's WebP
  and the PDF text during the art-readings continuation (2026-10-07). The page
  discusses the hollow chest/spine, cracked skin, cut Achilles tendon and heavy
  anklet; it does not identify the prose's author on the page. It establishes
  an archive relationship, not a chronology or an emotional sequel between the
  current red and white versions. Current titles remain unchanged. The viewer's
  earlier-study link preserves access to the dated source.

The supplied material did not establish an archive connection for Pain. Its
viewer therefore displays the original image without an invented relationship.

No standalone original EKSTAZ production track was found in the provided media
folders. The older music page contains curated external playlists, and the short
video has a car subject. Neither is presented as an original production track.
Original sound can be added when an identified recording is supplied.

Additional photographs and portfolio proposals were reviewed but not added to
the public site without reliable context. Draft proposals are not evidence of
executed commissions. Personal details from planning files remain out of public copy.
