The files are **not** in this repo: they are too large, and they are unreleased work. This catalog records what is in the archive and what each part could be used for, so a new session does not have to unpack it again. Cross-check names with `docs/ASSET-SOURCES.md` before using anything.

## Flags for Haris and Elton

1. **Old voice:** the 2020 banners and cards say "a new strain of exotic hype design" and "hype". The brand core says no manufactured hype. Keep these as images only if Elton wants them; do not reuse the wording.
2. **A third-party likeness:** `image3A8278_Glitch2.jpg` is a glitched portrait of a famous person. Do not use without a rights decision (STATUS D9).
3. **Portraits of Elton** (`elton.jpg`, `IMG_2464.JPG`) are personal photos; ask first.
4. **The T-shirt** has a different print ("NEVER BETTER" with roses) than the hoodie study's embroidered cross. Do not imply it is a current product.
5. **Magazine:** `magazine.pdf` here is 16 MB; the site serves a 4 MB edition. Check they match before swapping.
6. **Privacy:** the zip was opened to "Anyone with the link" for download. Confirm it was set back to Restricted.
