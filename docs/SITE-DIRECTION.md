# EKSTAZ website direction

This is implementation guidance, not additional copy for the website. It follows the
October 2026 brand core supplied by Haris and Elton and the EKSTAZ design system.

## Expression belongs to the visitor

EKSTAZ is about expression of the self in pursuit and spirit of ecstasy. It is the brand,
not one person's work. Experiences help people explore their own capacity to notice,
feel, choose and express. Elton's work can introduce this world without being required
in every experience, leaving room for Haris, Elton, visitors and future contributors.
Do not assign meaning to visitors' choices or equate effort with depth. Protect Elton's
supplied artwork, immutable identity assets and exact artist texts.

## Let the work lead

The site is another way to encounter the practice. Its moving signal is a visible part of
that encounter. Give the particle transformations a dedicated visual stage above each
chapter’s reading content, at full strength rather than dimmed into a faint background.
Text and original artwork have their own space below the stage. Enlarged artwork should
retain its original proportions and colours, with quiet controls, descriptive alternative
text and a direct link to the piece.

Use the existing title and image. Do not invent an artist statement, date, edition,
production stage or connection to another piece. A sketch, recording or garment belongs
alongside an artwork only when the source material supplies that relationship. Browsing the next
piece means continuing through the archive, not claiming the two pieces share a concept.

## Give the front door a readable journey

The homepage leads with View the collection and exposes Explore as an immediate
alternative. Three intact hoodie, tee and cap concepts appear in `#begin`, explicitly
labelled as concept visualizations, not finished garments or samples. Their links open
the corresponding collection piece with native fragment fallback. Unconfirmed details
stay unconfirmed; no price, availability or finished product claim is implied.

Every header uses Collection, Explore, About and Updates, visible on narrow screens.
About opens the collection's brand introduction. Print and Listen remain contextual
routes within Explore and the archive, separate from the primary navigation.

The homepage follows real content sections: introduction (`#begin`), Pain
(`#encounter`), optional reflection (`#reflection`), Cross and its hoodie study
(`#form`), optional ways to continue (`#expression`), and release updates (`#carry`).
Show the original artwork at the encounter and form checkpoints, including on narrow
screens, before asking someone to continue. Sticky progress links help visitors locate
themselves and revisit a section. Use native anchors; the text, images and routes remain
usable without JavaScript. Canvas state follows the actual DOM sections, rather than
assuming each stage occupies a particular number of viewport heights.

Preserve all six physical states: noise resolving into the wordmark, Pain rendered in
particles, the moving waveform, the stitched E, scattered carrier rings and the star mark.
Scrolling transforms one field through those states in the chapter order. Visual stages
must remain prominent on narrow screens as well as desktop, while the reading content,
original images and clear next steps remain unobscured. Clarity should come from the
composition and orientation, rather than suppressing the animation.

The reflection is a quiet question, not a form: no answer input, submission or response
collection. Recognition and acceptance describe possibilities, never prerequisites for
signup. Do not force a feeling, promise an emotional outcome or supply a definitive
interpretation of an artwork. Someone may look, carry, listen, read or leave without
declaring a response. Signup is an optional request for release updates.

The work page serves browsing and focused viewing, rather than repeating the homepage
manifesto; its outlined moving transmission strip and ambient field maintain the same
atmosphere. Its return route leads to `/#reflection`. The magazine returns to
`/#expression`, preserving its role as one optional way to continue.

Cross and the hoodie drawing share a documented motif: the study's notes call for an
embroidered red cross. Present that as a garment study, without implying a finished
garment or an available product.

## Identity marks and browser experiments

The original wordmark, standalone E and icon assets remain immutable. Use them as supplied:
no recolouring, outlining, redrawing or font substitutes. Full artwork views display the
source image unchanged.

The existing particle signal and numbered canvas marks are website experiments. Keep this
distinction in implementation: a particle rendering is a temporary visual treatment, and a
generated personal mark is not a new official logo or an original drawing by Elton. Do not
replace approved logo assets with either. Future experiments should be considered as
creative work in their own right, while the identity assets stay intact.

## Keep motion optional

Maintain the near-black ground, crimson accents, grain, Didone display type and monospace
controls. Motion should support the encounter rather than delay it. Pause the ambient
canvas while an artwork is open or the document is hidden, and freeze positions, opacity
and ring angles when reduced motion is requested. Reduced motion also removes reveals,
zoom-on-hover, ticker motion and page fades. Controls and navigation remain available.

## State what a number does

The waitlist number records arrival. A generated image can let someone save that record;
it does not establish rank, guarantee a garment, or create an edition of the artwork.
Describe any confirmed access policy separately and consistently across pages. Do not add
an unverified availability claim, countdown or scarcity message.

The public `?n=` mark preview is a demonstration, not evidence of registration. Only the
waitlist service can confirm a person's signup and number. A recovery feature should use
that service's confirmed record rather than treating a public URL as ownership.

## Preserve factual context

The magazine remains identified as the December 2021 archive. Show new studio fragments
only when there is actual material to publish, with the maker's supplied context. Do not
invent current recordings, progress updates or cross-disciplinary associations to make
the world appear busier.
