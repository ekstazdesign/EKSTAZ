# EKSTAZ — Brand core

Locked October 2026 by Haris and Elton. Every page, garment, track and decision checks against this.

**Meaning:** A state of recognition made tangible.

**Expression:** EKSTAZ is about expression of the self in pursuit and spirit of ecstasy. It is the brand, not one person's work. Experiences help people explore their own capacity to notice, feel, choose and express. Elton's work can introduce this world without being required in every experience, leaving room for Haris, Elton, visitors and future contributors. Do not assign meaning to visitors' choices or equate effort with depth. Elton's supplied artwork, identity assets and exact artist texts remain protected.

**Offer:** A way to carry a signal.
- Expression — you wear what you already feel.
- Reinforcement — you're recognised by others who carry it.
- Discovery — the work shows you what you respond to before you can name it.

**For:** People who feel before they explain, would rather be recognised by a few than seen by many, and move between worlds (art, sound, cars, ideas). A psychograph, not a place.

**Never:**
- Reshape the work to win a bigger audience.
- Explain it into slogans to make it easier to sell.
- Manufacture hype. No fake scarcity, no logo flexing. If people talk, it's because the work moved them.
